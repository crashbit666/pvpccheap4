Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

PVPC Cheap switches your smart plugs on and off at the hours when electricity is cheapest. Here we explain what data we use, what for, and what rights you have.

Who is the controller

The owner of the PVPC Cheap service. Contact: {{CONTACT}}.

What data we keep

  • Account: your username, password and email address. The password is never stored as such, only an irreversible hash (Argon2). The email is only used to verify it and to send you a link if you forget your password; we will not send you advertising.
  • Integrations: depending on the provider, the email and password of your Meross account and the session token it returns, or the address and access token of your Home Assistant. They are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and only used to control your devices on your behalf.
  • Devices: the name, type and identifier of your plugs and lights, and their power if you enter it.
  • Rules and history: the rules you create and the log of when each plug was switched on or off, so we can warn you if it fails and calculate how much you save.
  • Technical logs: the server records the requests it receives (IP address, time, path) to detect errors and abuse.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What we use it for and on what basis

Only to provide the service you ask for: scheduling and running your rules, showing you the state of your plugs, warning you about errors and calculating your savings. The legal basis is the performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Technical logs are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who it is shared with

  • Meross: to control the plugs, the server connects to the Meross cloud with your credentials. Meross processes this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Home Assistant: if you connect it, the server connects to your installation to switch your devices on and off.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): sends the account emails (email verification and password recovery). It receives your address and the text of the email.
  • Anthropic: if you write a rule in your own words (paid option), the text and the names of your devices are sent to Anthropic's Claude API so that it can suggest the configuration. Nothing else is sent, and only when you use this option.
  • Hosting: the server is at Hetzner Online GmbH, within the European Union, acting as data processor.

Electricity prices are obtained from Red Eléctrica (ESIOS); this request includes none of your data. Notifications are generated on the phone itself.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you delete it, everything is deleted from the database immediately. Encrypted backups are kept for a maximum of 14 days and then removed.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), Meross credentials are stored encrypted and passwords are hashed with Argon2. When you change your password, the sessions on your other phones are closed.

Your rights

You can access your data, rectify it, request its erasure, restriction or portability, and object to its processing, by writing to {{CONTACT}}. You can delete your account yourself from the app. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es) or the Catalan Data Protection Authority (Autoritat Catalana de Protecció de Dades, apdcat.gencat.cat).

Minors

The service is not intended for children under 14.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
