The service

PVPC Cheap checks the hourly PVPC electricity price and switches your smart plugs on or off according to the rules you set up. To do so, you need to connect your account with a supported manufacturer (Meross) or your Home Assistant.

Your account

You are responsible for keeping your password secret and for using the service only with plugs that are yours or that you have permission to control.

Appropriate use

Do not connect to a scheduled plug any appliance that could be dangerous if it is switched on or off with nobody around, nor any medical or safety device. Check that the plug can handle the appliance's power.

Availability and limitations

We do our best to run the rules on time, but the service depends on the Internet, on the manufacturer's cloud and on Red Eléctrica publishing the prices. There may be interruptions, delays or errors. If a plug does not respond, the service retries and warns you, but we cannot guarantee that it will always switch on or off. The savings shown in the app are an estimate.

Meross plugs are controlled through their cloud, which is not an official API: if Meross changes it, it may stop working until we adapt to it.
