Delete your PVPC Cheap account

Last updated: 22 October 2026

From the app

  1. Open PVPC Cheap and sign in with your username.
  2. In Devices, tap the account icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Before it is deleted, any plugs that a rule has switched on are switched off.

What is deleted

Everything, straight away: the account, the Meross credentials, the devices, the rules and the history. Encrypted backups disappear within a maximum of 14 days. Your Meross account itself is not touched.

If you cannot sign in to the app

Write to {{CONTACT}} from the account's email address or giving its username, and we will delete it.
